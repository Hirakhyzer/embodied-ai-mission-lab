    Design principles
    • Hardware-neutralMissions describe capabilities, not vendors.
    • Safety before actionEvery movement passes through a safety decision.
    • ObservablePlanning and execution create inspectable events.
    • ExtensibleSensors, planners, and robot adapters can become plugins.
